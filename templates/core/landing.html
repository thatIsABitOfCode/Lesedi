What you can do today

Living Safety Plan

Write your plan, choose a primary and backup responder, and see gentle suggestions to strengthen it.

Invite people, give each one only the permissions you choose, and take them back at any time.

Guardian Mode

Notify the people you authorised, track who has acknowledged, and try your backup if the first person does not respond.

Evidence Vault

Keep dated notes and files encrypted and private. Export a pack only when you choose.

What LESEDI cannot do

  • It does not contact police or emergency services.
  • An acknowledgement means a person saw an alert, not that they are on their way.
  • It cannot find spyware or guarantee that a device is private.
  • This version is a development build: messages are simulated.
