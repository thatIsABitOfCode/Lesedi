Worried about someone?

If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services now. This form is not an emergency line.

What happens when you send this

  • Only moderators and you can see it. It is never published and never appears in the community feed.
  • LESEDI does not contact the person you are worried about, anyone close to them, the police or any other authority.
  • It gives you no access to their account, location, evidence or plans, and gives them no sign that you wrote.
  • A moderator may add a short note, for example a pointer to support services. You can read it under "My reports". Nobody promises a response time.
