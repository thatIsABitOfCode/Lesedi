If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services. This form is not an emergency line and nobody is alerted when you send it.

  • A moderator reads your report first. Other people only ever see a short summary the moderator writes.
  • Your name is never shown to other users.
  • Do not include names, phone numbers, ID numbers, addresses or links. Describe the situation, not the person.
  • LESEDI does not verify reports and does not contact the police or anyone else about them.
